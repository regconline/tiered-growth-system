Opening a new practice is a huge step. You've handled registration, premises, equipment and staff — now you need patients. The first 90 days set the tone for growth, and a clear digital plan means you're not starting from zero on opening day.

## Before you open (weeks −6 to 0)

### Build your brand foundations

- **Practice name** that's easy to say, spell and search.
- **Logo and colour palette** that look professional and calm.
- **Consistent details** — the exact name, address and phone number you'll use everywhere.

### Secure your online assets

- Register your domain (.co.za) and a professional email address.
- Reserve social media handles on Facebook and Instagram.
- Create your Google Business Profile and start verification early — it can take time.

### Launch a website before opening day

Even a compact website with five key pages is enough to start:

1. Home — who you are, what you treat, where you are.
2. About — practitioner bios, qualifications and registration.
3. Services — what you offer.
4. Medical aid & fees.
5. Contact — map, hours, WhatsApp and booking request form.

Add a privacy policy and POPIA-compliant forms from day one. See [website must-haves](/blog/healthcare-website-must-haves/).

## Month 1: Get found

- **Complete your Google Business Profile** with categories, services, photos and opening date.
- **List your practice** in relevant medical directories and your professional association.
- **Tell your network** — colleagues, previous patients (where appropriate), community organisations.
- **Introduce yourself to referrers** — nearby GPs, specialists, pharmacies and allied health practitioners.
- **Start Google Ads** for your key services in a tight radius, so you're visible immediately while SEO builds.

## Month 2: Build trust

- **Start asking for reviews** from every patient. Early reviews make a big difference for new practices. Use our [ethical review guide](/blog/get-more-google-reviews-medical-practice/).
- **Post consistently on social media** — two to three times a week. Introduce the team, show the practice and share health tips.
- **Publish your first articles** answering common patient questions in your speciality.
- **Set up WhatsApp Business** with quick replies and reminders.

## Month 3: Optimise and grow

- **Review your data** — which channels bring enquiries? What do patients ask most?
- **Improve landing pages** for your best-performing services.
- **Expand content** around your highest-value services.
- **Introduce recall reminders** so new patients come back.
- **Plan community visibility** — health talks, school or workplace partnerships.

## Your launch budget priorities

If budget is tight, invest in this order:

1. Google Business Profile (free) and a professional website.
2. Reviews system (low cost, high impact).
3. Google Ads in a small radius for core services.
4. Social media and content.

## Common launch mistakes

- Waiting until after opening to build the website.
- Inconsistent practice details across Google, directories and signage.
- No system for asking for reviews.
- Generic stock photos instead of your real practice.
- Marketing claims that breach HPCSA rules — keep every claim factual and verifiable.

## Frequently asked questions

**When should I start marketing a new practice?**
Ideally six weeks or more before opening, so your website and Google profile are live and verified on day one.

**How quickly can a new practice appear on Google Maps?**
Once your profile is verified and complete, you can appear within days — but ranking prominently takes reviews and time.

**Should a new practice use Google Ads?**
Often yes. Ads provide visibility while you build reviews and SEO. Keep the radius tight and track enquiries.
